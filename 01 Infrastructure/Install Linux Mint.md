## Prerequisite

The virtual machine is created and its image is attached: [[Create a VMware Virtual Machine]].

## Installation

1. Press Enter to avoid waiting, otherwise the machine starts automatically after a few seconds.
2. To install Linux, click this CD:

![[install-linux-mint--fig-01--install-cd-icon.png]]
*the CD to click to start the installation*

3. Choose the language of the system, then click **Continue**.
4. Choose the keyboard language. You can add several languages. Click **Continue**.
5. Skip this installation step, because it takes time. You can install it later when you want.
6. You have nothing on your disk, because you are renting an empty space on your physical machine, so nothing will be deleted.
7. Choose your location.
8. Fill in the necessary information and press **Continue**.
9. Wait until the installation finishes, then restart the machine.
